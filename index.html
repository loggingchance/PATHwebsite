The Problem
You're working hard. But do you know if you're making money?

You know your gross revenue. You know your stumpage checks. But do you know your true cost of production—by job, by week, by year? Do you know your return on investment?

Most operators don't—and it's costing them. Without real financial data on each job, you can't tell a good job from a bad one until it's too late.

You're "busy" but the account isn't growing

Full days, full trucks, full schedule—but at year end the profit doesn't match the effort. The problem isn't how hard you work. It's which costs you can't see.

You quote jobs on gut feel

Without knowing your real costs, real profit, and real ROI, every job estimate is a guess. Some jobs look great on gross revenue and bleed you on net. You won't know until you're deep in.

Equipment decisions are a coin flip

Should you repair or replace? Add a machine or run leaner? Without knowing your true operating costs, depreciation, and ROI, every capital decision is a gamble.

The Insight
You can't improve what you can't measure.

Your actual costs, your real profit, and your return on investment—by week, by year, and most importantly, for each individual job—are the numbers that tell you whether your business is working.

Without them, every decision is a guess. With them, you can price jobs with confidence, compare one job against another, and answer the question every logging business owner needs answered: is this job worth doing?

PATH 4.0 calculates all of it—costs, profit, and ROI—for any time frame, any job, any scenario you want to run.

How It Works
Four steps. Your real numbers. Real answers.

PATH walks you through a structured process to build your cost picture from the ground up.

1

Overhead

Insurance, repairs, office, vehicles, supplies—the annual cost of keeping your business alive whether you produce or not. Your tax return has most of these numbers.

2

Business Specs

Equipment values, loans, payroll, fuel, and weeks of production. PATH calculates your weekly depreciation, compares it to your payments, and picks the higher of the two—giving you a conservative, honest cost picture.

3

Products

List what you produce and what you net per load—gross price minus stumpage minus trucking. The money that actually stays with your business.

4

Results

Select a time frame—one week, a whole job, or a full year. Enter production. See your profit or loss and your return on investment, side by side.

Exclusive Feature
PATHWAYS

The built-in scenario engine that lets you ask "what if?" and get an answer in seconds.

Once your baseline numbers are in place, PATHWAYS puts slider controls on the variables that move your bottom line—fuel price, production rate, payroll, overhead. Drag a slider and watch your profit and ROI recalculate in real time.

No spreadsheets. No formulas. No guesswork. You change a variable, you see the result—instantly.

Fuel Price Sensitivity

Diesel jumped 40 cents? Slide it and see exactly how it hits your costs, profit, and ROI.

Production Rate Modeling

What if you ran one more load per day? Or lost two weeks to mud season? See the P&L shift before it happens.

Payroll & Overhead Scenarios

Thinking about adding a crew member? Weighing a new insurance quote? Model the impact before you commit.

Job Comparison

Run the same cost structure against different jobs. Some that look great on gross revenue look very different when you see the net.
